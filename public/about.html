How member research works

Active Research members can open a research brief with a question, context and expected deliverable.

A member with an active Researcher credential can accept the request, submit findings and respond to revisions. The original requester reviews the findings and accepts completed work or asks for revisions.

Membership and credentials

The workspace supports membership, renewal, contributor applications and membership-linked credentials. Membership and role checks apply to protected actions.
